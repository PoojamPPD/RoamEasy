About RoamEasy

RoamEasy is a concept tourism website designed for young, budget-conscious travellers who want to discover and plan a weekend trip quickly, without digging through long menus or scattered information. This prototype demonstrates the core flow — discover, explore, view a destination and plan a trip — for usability testing.
